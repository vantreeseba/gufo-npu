# Qwen3.6-35B-A3B experiments

Short retained/rejected decisions; the runs behind them are in
[BENCHMARKS.md](BENCHMARKS.md), [QUALITY.md](QUALITY.md) and
[the MoE spike](../../research/NPU-MOE.md).

| Experiment | Decision / qualification |
| --- | --- |
| Expert selection by gathers with patched DDR offsets | Retained: two calls per layer (gate + up over 9 experts, then down with K = 9 × 512), their offsets and router scales rewritten in the instruction stream after CPU routing: 414 µs per layer in a 40-layer chain, against 948 µs for one run per expert matrix. |

| MTP speculation with two-token passes over the expert union | Retained: a pair reads the dense weights once and the union of both tokens' experts (8 to 16 routed plus the shared one) once; each token weighs the other's experts 0 and experts sum in id order, so a two-token pass is bit-identical to two one-token passes and speculative output equals plain greedy. 26.0–28.9 tok/s at short and ~1k context with 75–83% of drafts kept, against 18.7–20.3 without and FLM's 16.9–17.1. The prompt runs two tokens per pass: 942 tokens in 31 s instead of 48 s. |

| Queuing a chain's next call's weights before the previous call finishes (the next projection's while the down gather drains; the down gather's first two segment groups behind gate + up's last) | Rejected: no change, same outputs. Next projection alone: deltanet chain 643–646 µs against 643–645; with the down groups too, on the packed RTP word: 618.6–625.4 against 619.4–620.6, two tokens at 13 experts 791.5–796.4 against 790.3–791.5. Weight arrival does not stall a call boundary. |

Per call in a 20-call runlist (best of 10, two runs): gate + up 280–282 → 245 µs at 9 experts (39 → 44.6 GB/s), 297–301 → 262–264 at 9 for two tokens, 398–404 → 346–349 at 13, 500–502 → 434–437 at 17; down unchanged (136–138 µs); a layer's pair 413–418 → 382 µs. | Error-minimizing fit for requantized weights (llama.cpp's Q4_K search, weights av + &#124;w&#124;) | Retained for this model (`qwen35::Q4nxFit` names `q4nx::Fit::kSearch` for its decoder, MTP block and draft head; the NPU prefill converts those buffers): on 1,515 held-out positions KL against the GGUF falls 24–27% on every weight set (all matrices: 0.0394 → 0.0301, top-1 91.95% → 93.07%; oracle top-1 against llama.cpp 260 → 271 of 301).
